Support

Questions about Arbor, answered in plain words. If yours isn’t here, write to us.

Write to usarborcuratorapp+support@gmail.comIn Arbor, you can also send us a message from Settings › Support.

Getting started

What is Arbor?

An app for iPhone, iPad and Mac that organizes your screenshots and photos. It reads what’s in each one, gives it a name you’d recognize, and files it into the places you make.

What is a Willow?

Your Forest holds everything. Willows are the big groups, like Recipes or Travel. Branches grow inside them, like Desserts or Lisbon.

Which devices does it run on?

Apple devices only: iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or later, and a Mac with macOS 26.5 or later.

Is Arbor on Android or Windows?

Not yet. Arbor is made for Apple devices.

Is it in other languages?

Arbor is in English and Spanish.

Your photos

Does Arbor upload anything?

Not on Basic: everything stays on your device. With Plus, everything is scrambled on your device before it goes to Arbor’s cloud, so nobody at Arbor can see it.

How is it backed up?

With Plus, your Forest is backed up in Arbor’s cloud. On a new device, sign in with the same Apple or Google account and everything comes back. Basic doesn’t back up, so keep your device’s own backup on.

What happens to the originals in Photos?

On iPhone and iPad, the originals stay in Photos if you want them to. On the Mac, they move into Arbor so they aren’t in two places, and your Mac asks first.

Can I take everything back out?

Always. On the Mac, every Willow and Branch is an ordinary folder. On iPhone and iPad, Save puts a copy in Files.

Does it work without the internet?

Reading, naming and filing all happen on your device. Checking a website from a link, syncing and backup need a connection.

Does Arbor learn the wrong things?

It learns from your fixes, and you can see everything it has learned in Learning. Open any of it to change it, or make Arbor forget it.

Basic and Plus

What’s free, and what comes with Plus?

Basic is free: sorting, naming and filing, learning from your fixes, and up to 500 photos on one device. Plus adds no limit, the same Forest on all your devices, backup, tasks and calendar events from your photos, finding the same content saved twice, Teaching Guides and more.

How much is Plus, and is there a free trial?

Plus is free for your first 7 days, then $6.99 a month or $49.99 a year. Cancel before the 7 days end and you pay nothing.

Does Plus cover all my devices?

Yes. One subscription covers your iPhone, iPad and Mac. If Plus doesn’t show on another device, choose Restore purchase on Arbor’s Upgrade screen.

How do I cancel?

On iPhone or iPad, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. On the Mac, open the App Store, click your name, then Account Settings › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the next renewal.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles every purchase, so refunds go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What happens if I stop Plus?

Nothing is deleted. Past 500, the first 500 you added keep working and the rest stay visible but locked until you upgrade again or get back under 500. Locked ones can still be opened, saved, shared or deleted.

Can my family share Plus?

Not yet. Plus is for one person, on all of that person’s devices.

Your account

How do I delete my account?

In Arbor, open Settings › Account & Storage › Delete my account and data. That removes your account and every backed-up file in Arbor’s cloud. Photos on your devices stay until you delete them.
